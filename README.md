# Prépa TEF TCF

Practise the TEF and TCF Canada French exams in their real format, before paying $390 for the real one.

**Live app:** [dist-orpin-six-42.vercel.app](https://dist-orpin-six-42.vercel.app)

<img src="apps/mobile/web-qr.png" alt="QR code to open the live app" width="160">

## What I did

- **Built the whole product with AI coding agents:** the app, the website, the demo video and the pitch deck. I don't write code by hand. I direct the agents, review every change and test on real devices.
- **Took it to two competitions:** I built it for the [Meta AI Developer Hackathon](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon) (result: _announced Nov 9, 2026_), then brought it to York's **UNHack**, where our team reached the **final 42 teams**.
- **Led and mentored a student team:** four students, from first year to fourth year. I set the direction, explained the product, coached the pitch, and gave everyone a clear role on stage and in Q&A. My goal was to help them gain confidence.
