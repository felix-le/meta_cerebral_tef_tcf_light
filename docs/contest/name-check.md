## Points for the owner
- The name contains **TEF**, which is a registered Canadian mark of CCI Paris in class 41 (education). Many prep
  products use "TEF" and "TCF" to describe what they prepare for, but whether that is allowed for this name is a
  question for a lawyer.
- "TCF TEF Prep" and "PrepMyTEF / PrepMyTCF" are close in wording and in market.
- The `.com`, `.ca` and `.fr` domains for `prepateftcf` looked free on 2026-10-27. Nothing has been registered.
